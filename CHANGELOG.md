# SNMP neighbours plugin

## 1.1
- Reads the switches' MAC tables too (BRIDGE-MIB and Q-BRIDGE-MIB, per VLAN): with labdex 1.24, Discovery › Where is finds the switch port of any MAC or IP. Setting *Read MAC tables* (on by default).

- LLDP-MIB and CISCO-CDP-MIB over SNMP v2c and v3; neighbours become suggested cables.
